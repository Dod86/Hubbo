Privacy Policy — Hubbo

Version: 1.4 · Last updated: 30 September 2026 · Italiano

1. Who runs Hubbo

Hubbo is an app for managing your personal subscriptions, available as an Android app and as a website (PWA). Data controller: Ernesto Nigro. Privacy contact: devdod86@gmail.com.

2. Data you enter

Hubbo lets you enter information about your subscriptions, such as the service name, price, currency, billing frequency, renewal dates, notes, subscription status and payment history. It also stores your preferences (language, country, currency, theme, reminder settings). This data is saved on your device and does not require creating a Hubbo account. It is not sent to the controller or to any Hubbo server.

3. External services

Hubbo has no server of its own and does not collect your data on servers run by the controller. Some features, however, make requests to third-party services. With every request, the provider may receive the technical information needed to connect, such as your IP address and standard request data, and handles it under its own policies:

  • Exchange rates: up-to-date rates from the Frankfurter service (European Central Bank data). Your subscriptions are not sent in the request.
  • Service catalogue and offers: downloaded from GitHub Pages. Your subscriptions are not sent in the request.
  • Premium purchase: if you buy Premium, the payment is handled by Google Play under its own policies. Hubbo does not receive or store your card or account details: it only asks Google Play whether your Google account has purchased Premium, in order to turn it on or back on.
  • Cancellation help: if you tap the option that helps you cancel a subscription, Hubbo opens a Google search for that service in your browser. The search text contains the name of the service you tapped, and you are then subject to Google's policies.

Service logos, typefaces and page styling are bundled inside the app: they are not downloaded from external services and cause no network request.

4. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Hubbo schedules local reminders on your device for upcoming renewals, even when the app is closed. Notifications are generated by the app on your phone and do not go through any server run by the controller. In the Android app the system asks for permission to send notifications, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings. Hubbo does not request access to your contacts, SMS, location, microphone or camera.

5. Backup and export

You can manually export a backup of your data. The exported file is yours to manage and can be shared with other apps or services of your choice.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you edit it, delete it, use the full-deletion function or uninstall the app. Hubbo includes a function to delete all of the app's local data. Because the controller does not hold your data, deleting it on your device is enough to remove it.

7. Security

Communications with the external services used by the app take place over HTTPS whenever the service supports it. Data stored locally is subject to the protections available on your device.

8. Purchases (Hubbo Premium)

If you choose to buy Hubbo Premium, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. Hubbo does not receive or store your card or payment-method details and only uses the purchase confirmation to unlock the features.

9. Sharing and advertising

Hubbo contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools, and does not sell your personal data. Any future integration will be reflected in this policy and in the Google Play Data safety declaration.

10. Your rights

Since your subscription data lives only on your device, you can access, correct, export and delete it yourself from within the app. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland you also have the rights set out in data-protection law (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority; for Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. To exercise a right, write to the contact below.

11. Children

Hubbo is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age) and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when Hubbo's features or the services it uses change. The update date is shown at the top of the page.

13. Contact

For privacy questions: devdod86@gmail.com.
